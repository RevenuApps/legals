❓ Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Run left and right and jump up the platforms. The faster you run, the higher you jump. Clear more than one floor in a single leap to build a combo and multiply your points.

I lost my gems or progress?

Your progress and gems are saved on your device. Email us at apps@nafes.net describing the issue and we'll help.

How do I unlock new characters?

Collect gems while you play and spend them in the shop to unlock new icy heroes and star characters.

I have a problem with a purchase?

Email us at apps@nafes.net with the purchase details and we'll sort it out as fast as we can.
