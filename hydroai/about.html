About us

HydroAI is a water-reminder app built on one simple idea: break laziness, not cheating.

What it does

  • Reminders that fit your day: your active hours and days, and quiet times for lunch or meetings.
  • An alarm that keeps ringing until you drink, with an optional photo check that looks for a cup or bottle.
  • Your daily goal, and a different goal for any weekday if you want one.
  • A clear view of your day and your week.
  • Eight languages: English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese and Russian.

What we care about

  • Private by design. Your drink log stays on your phone, and the photo check runs on the device and deletes the picture straight away.
  • Never trapped. You can always confirm without a photo, snooze, or turn the alarm off.
  • Gentle, not pushy. Reminders follow your schedule, not ours.
