Frequently asked questions

How do I add a product to my list?
Open the catalog, choose a category, then add the products you want to your needs list.

How do I record a purchase?
From the needs list, tap an item to move it to purchased, where you can add price and quantity.

Is my data saved?
Yes, your list and purchases are stored on your device. For details see the Privacy Policy.
